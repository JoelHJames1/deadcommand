Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Applies to the Dead Command app for iPhone and iPad

The short version: Dead Command has no accounts and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Your progress is stored on your own device. We never sell your data. Information leaves your device only for Game Center leaderboards, online co-op, purchases, optional reward videos and anonymous gameplay analytics, and it is handled by Apple and Unity.

Information stored on your device

Your coins, unlocked weapons and uniforms, outfit, callsign, best scores and daily-reward streak are saved locally on your device. We do not receive or store this information on our own servers. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best Single Player and Co-op scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, where they appear with your Game Center nickname. This is provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in your device Settings at any time.

Online co-op

Co-op play uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay) provided by Unity Technologies. When you host, join or quick-match a squad, the app signs in anonymously with a randomly generated player ID (no name, email or phone number), and Unity's servers relay game data between you and your partner. Unity processes your IP address and basic device and connection information to provide this service. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

During a co-op match your partner sees your soldier, your outfit and the callsign you chose. Pick a callsign that does not contain personal information.

Purchases

Coin packs and the Commander Pack are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment card or Apple ID.

Reward videos (ads)

The game never shows an ad on its own. You can choose to watch a short video to earn free coins, double the coins from a run, or get a revive. These videos are served by Unity LevelPlay (with the Unity Ads network), provided by Unity Technologies. To serve a video, measure that it was watched and prevent fraud, Unity collects your device's vendor identifier, an approximate location derived from your IP address, basic device and app information, and ad interaction data. The app does not ask for permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and the videos are not personalized using it. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Gameplay analytics

To learn how the game is played and balance it, the app sends anonymous gameplay events (for example, the wave you reached, your score, backup called, a coin pack bought or a reward video watched) to Unity Analytics, linked to the same random player ID used for co-op. It does not include your name, email or Apple ID. You can switch this off at any time with Settings › Share Analytics in the game. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no request for the advertising identifier.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information for marketing.
  • No ads you didn't choose to watch.
  • No collection of your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera.

Children

Dead Command is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and your choices

Because your game data lives on your device, you control it: delete the app to remove it. Anonymous co-op sign-in data, analytics events, ad data and Game Center scores are retained by Unity and Apple under their own policies. Turning off Share Analytics stops new analytics events from being sent. To ask a question or make a request about your data, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy or the game? Send us a message (a free GitHub account is required), or see the Support page.
